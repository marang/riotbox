## 13. Current Repo Status

Implemented-profile ownership lives in [automated QA](./automated_qa.md),
[artifact/export proof](./manifests_and_artifacts.md), and [human
review](./listening_review.md). Current deterministic render, correlation,
timing, replay/recovery, and bounded export evidence does not prove automatic
recovery, host-audio soak, broad crash recovery, or endurance. General
full-corpus rendering, host capture, CI-wide recipe correlation, and calibrated
human-rubric automation remain future work.

## 14. Near-Term Build Order

For a new audible mechanism, follow the
[P023 audible-delivery plan](../../plans/p023_audible_delivery_course_correction.md):
first use one registered Development source and at most three materially
distinct variants to establish provisional musician value. Stop weak directions
without building promotion machinery. Only a kept and frozen mechanism proceeds
to product-spine integration, the applicable source matrix, replay/callback and
exact-output proof, and formal listening review.

Metric, manifest, fixture, and CI expansion is subordinate to a named product
claim or measured regression gap. Existing shared behavior and regressions keep
their normal gates; Holdout, realtime, source, and release boundaries are
unchanged.

## 15. Success Condition

Catch broken audio, reproduce exact comparisons, hear validated cases, and turn
recurring weakness into the smallest useful gate or product fix.
