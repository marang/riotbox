# Riotbox Rave-Punk Production

## Operating Rule

Shape Riotbox toward hostile energy, transformed samples, memorable hooks,
physical drums, controlled chaos, and immediate live impact—not imitation of a
living artist or recreation of released material.

For audible character, pattern, slice, loop, preset, demo, drum/bass, or
performance-control work, read and apply the direct
[Taste Rubric](references/taste-rubric.md).

## Contract Routes

- Use the [audio-QA router](../../../docs/specs/audio_qa_workflow_spec.md) for
  Golden Path, source matrices, holdouts, fallback, primitive, pressure,
  playback, and human-verdict gates.
- Use [Percussive Force and Beat Impact](../../../docs/engineering/percussive_force_and_beat_impact.md)
  for every typed hardness claim.
- Use `../riotbox-listening-review/SKILL.md` before human playback or verdict.

Keep one clear hook and hardest element ahead of decorative variety. Prefer
controls that change the room immediately, and treat polite, hookless,
placeholder, source-collapsed, or technically active but unplayable output as a
product failure rather than a presentational problem.

Every taste critique must name one concrete implementation or QA follow-up.
