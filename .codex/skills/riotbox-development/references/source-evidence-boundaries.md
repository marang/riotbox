# Source Evidence Boundaries

Use this reference for source-derived claims, one-off Development acquisition,
product primitives, holdout work, or commercial-reference handling. Read the
applicable source/provenance sections of the
[audio-QA router](../../../../docs/specs/audio_qa_workflow_spec.md) before
acting; that spec and its active versioned manifests own exact schemas, quotas,
partitions, and gates.

## Claim Boundary

A source-derived or intelligent claim must connect real source evidence to a
musical decision, store that decision in the product spine, render an audible
consequence, and satisfy the active stability, diversity, and human-review
contract. Hardcoded phrases, fixed templates, scripted arcs, hashes, and
fingerprint-only mutations remain controls or scaffolds until this chain exists.

When trusted source-backed material is unavailable, fail closed to visible
unavailable/degraded state or silence. Musical replacement audio is forbidden
on product paths; diagnostic controls must remain explicit and non-product.

A fixed typed versioned instrument primitive may be valid product output only
when an explicit committed performer gesture owns it and the shared validator
accepts its provenance and affected product paths. It remains
`primitive_renderer`: never automatic fallback, source-selected composition, or
source-intelligence proof. Declare truthful source modulation separately.

## Development Acquisition

One-off source acquisition is operational work, not a Riotbox subsystem:

- use an exact allowlist, standard network client, Riotbox-scoped OS keyring,
  bounded local access, and existing rights/hash/header validation
- do not search, preview, substitute, retry, or discover directories beyond the
  currently authorized boundary
- keep credentials, ignored source material, and acquisition artifacts out of
  terminal output and Git
- if downloader, transaction, reconciliation, race/crash, schema, or validator
  scaffolding begins to grow before a real source is qualified, stop, remove the
  excess, and return to the named audible follow-up

## Development Versus Holdout

Development matrices, Golden Path taste sources, unseen holdouts, and reserve
holdouts answer different questions. Never count familiar tuning material as
fresh acceptance evidence or use holdout results to select an algorithm or its
constants.

Before any selected-file open, enforce the active identity/path/hash exclusion
against protected partitions without reading protected audio. Active holdout
audio may not be opened, read, hashed, rendered, classified, or played except
at the phase explicitly authorized by the owning contract. Do not glob or
discover the source directory. If holdout evidence informs later implementation,
record it as consumed and rotate replacement evidence before a fresh claim.

## Commercial References

Commercial recordings are local, ignored, uncommitted listening and measurement
coordinates only. They are never Riotbox product sources, fixtures, generated
assets, review-pack content, corpus entries, or redistribution material. Reject
their identities, paths, and flags from source/holdout admission before access.
